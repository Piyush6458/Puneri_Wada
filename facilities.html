Facilities

Shooting Spaces & Amenities

Devran Wada offers diverse spaces and facilities to support your creative production

Traditional Chowk (Courtyard)

The central courtyard is the heart of the wada, featuring traditional stone flooring and surrounded by wooden pillars. Perfect for wide shots, group scenes, and capturing the authentic wada atmosphere. Natural lighting throughout the day creates beautiful shadows and ambiance.

Wooden Pillars & Carved Doors

Intricately carved wooden pillars and traditional doors provide stunning architectural details. These elements add depth and character to photographs and videos, offering close-up opportunities and beautiful framing options.

Interior Rooms

Multiple rooms with traditional architecture and rustic charm. These spaces can be used for intimate scenes, indoor shoots, and provide shelter for equipment and crew during breaks.

Outdoor Rural Settings

Surrounding farmland and rural landscape offer diverse outdoor shooting locations. Capture the essence of village life with natural greenery, traditional farming elements, and scenic views.

Terrace & Elevated Views

Access to elevated areas provides panoramic views of the surrounding countryside and Sinhagad Fort. Ideal for aerial-perspective shots, sunrise/sunset photography, and establishing shots.

Parking & Equipment Space

Ample parking space for production vehicles and equipment trucks. Designated areas for setting up equipment, makeup stations, and crew facilities.
